# Prompts for URL-to-video tools

Five 30-second vertical videos, one per feature, for tools that record the site and add a voiceover. Paste the brand block first, then one prompt.

## Brand block (prefix every prompt)

Brand: SmartSarmaya, a free AI research site for the Pakistan Stock Exchange. Website: https://www.smartsarmaya.com. Logo: https://www.smartsarmaya.com/images/logo-512.png. Colours: navy #0B132B background, emerald #10B981 accent, white text. Font: DM Sans or similar clean sans-serif. Tone: calm, factual, no hype, no shouting. Format: vertical 9:16, 30 seconds, captions on screen (most viewers watch muted), English voiceover in a neutral accent, light background music, no stock footage of traders shouting or piles of cash. Always end with the on-screen line "Educational only. Not financial advice." Never use the words "guaranteed", "predict", "sure shot" or "profit".

On SmartSarmaya, every company page answers in its first sentence. PPL: compliant. OGDC: compliant. HBL: not compliant. Three hundred and twenty-two of five hundred and forty-six listed companies currently pass. Search any symbol, free, no account. SmartSarmaya dot com. Educational only, not financial advice."

Do not describe index membership as a fatwa or religious ruling; call it a screen.

## 3 · Daily market brief

## 5 · Dividend and tax calculators

Make a 30-second vertical video explaining what a PSX dividend actually pays, using the free calculator.

Screen recording: open https://www.smartsarmaya.com/calculators, scroll to "Dividend income and yield", enter share price 228, dividend per share 6, shares held 400, with "Filer" selected. Show the results: gross Rs 2,400, withholding Rs 360, net Rs 2,040, yield 2.6%. Then switch to "Non-filer" and show withholding change to 30%. Briefly pan over the capital-gains-tax calculator above it.

Scenes: 0–5 s hook, text: "PPL declared a 60% dividend. That is NOT 60% of the price." 5–10 s text: "PSX dividends are quoted on the Rs 10 par value. 60% = Rs 6 per share." 10–24 s the calculator recording with captions "Gross", "Withholding 15% (filer)", "Net", "Yield". 24–30 s CTA: "Dividend, capital-gains and growth calculators, free → smartsarmaya.com/calculators".

Voiceover: "A sixty percent dividend sounds huge. It isn't sixty percent of the price. PSX dividends are quoted on the ten-rupee par value, so sixty percent means six rupees per share. Four hundred shares: two thousand four hundred rupees gross. Filers lose fifteen percent to withholding, non-filers thirty. Net: two thousand and forty rupees, a two-point-six percent yield at today's price. Run your own numbers on SmartSarmaya's free calculators. Educational only, not tax advice."

Mention that the shares must be held before the book-closure date to receive the dividend.
