## 2 · Dividend calculator — `feature-dividend-PPL.jpg`

**PPL declared a 60% final dividend. What does that actually pay you?**

Not 60% of the price. PSX dividends are quoted on the Rs 10 par value, so 60% = Rs 6 per share.

400 shares × Rs 6 = Rs 2,400 gross
Withholding tax, filer 15% = − Rs 360
Paid to you = Rs 2,040
Yield at Rs 228 = 2.6%

Non-filers pay 30% withholding, so being on the ATL is worth sorting out before dividend season.
