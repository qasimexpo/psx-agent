# Off-site publishing

Two pieces, each written once for one destination. They are deliberately not copies of anything on the site, so publishing them does not create duplicate content.

| File | Where | Why there |
|---|---|---|
| `01-explainer-for-finance-outlets.md` | ProPakistani, Profit, Business Recorder op-ed, Mettis | Their readers are the target audience; one editorial link from any of them is worth more than a hundred directory listings |
| `02-builder-post-linkedin-medium.md` | LinkedIn article → Medium/dev.to with canonical | Builder audience, brand mentions, and LinkedIn distribution to Pakistani finance and tech networks |

## Rules that keep this from backfiring

1. **One destination per piece.** If ProPakistani takes the explainer, do not also send it to Profit. Write a new one.
2. **Never paste a site guide onto another site.** Google ignores the copy and may prefer the larger site's version over yours.
3. **Set a canonical when syndicating.** Medium and dev.to both let you set the canonical URL to the first place it was published. LinkedIn does not; publish there first or accept it as the original.
4. **Links in the piece:** one to the home page, at most one to a specific guide, in prose, not a link list. Editors strip anything that reads as SEO.
5. **No payment for placement.** Paid links have to be marked `sponsored`, and unmarked ones fall under Google's link-spam policy.
6. **Tag links with UTM** so you can see in GA4 which placement sent people: `?utm_source=propakistani&utm_medium=article&utm_campaign=halal-explainer`.

## Where PSX investors actually are

For distribution beyond articles, in rough order of usefulness:

- **Facebook groups** for PSX investors (several with 100k+ members). Share the daily brief with a one-line summary, not a link alone. Group rules often ban self-promotion; a brief with real numbers is usually welcomed, a "check out my site" is not.
- **X/Twitter** Pakistani fintwit. The pipeline already posts there once credentials are set.
- **WhatsApp Channels and Telegram** as a broadcast-only channel for the closing brief. Note the About page currently says the site does not run a Telegram channel; update that wording if you start one, and keep it broadcast-only so it never becomes a "signal group".
- **YouTube Shorts / Instagram Reels**: a 30-second read of the closing brief over the KSE-100 chart, daily. The pipeline's share card is the thumbnail.
- **Reddit** r/PakistanStockExchange and r/pakistan finance threads: answer questions with the company page as the citation.
- **Broker newsletters and university finance societies**: offer the guides as free reading material.
